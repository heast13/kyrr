kyrr (Old Norse): still, quiet, at rest.

What it does

Removes background noise

Keyboard clatter, fans and traffic are filtered out of your microphone before anyone hears them. Good for calls, streams and recordings, in every app: Teams, Zoom, Discord, OBS, your browser.

Improves your voice

Evens out loud and quiet parts, softens harsh S sounds and cuts low rumble. It is on from the start; sliders in Settings if you want to tune it.

Types what you say

Hold F9, speak, let go: your words appear wherever your cursor is, in an email, a document or a chat. German and English. Say “new line” for a line break.

How it works

  1. Install kyrr. It sets itself up the first time it starts.
  2. Use your microphone as always. kyrr sits between the microphone and your apps, so they get the cleaned sound without any setting. On Linux it adds a microphone called “kyrr” and makes it your default.
  3. To dictate, hold F9 and talk. The first time, kyrr downloads the speech model once (up to 900 MB). After that it all runs on your PC.
  4. Want proof? Settings has a ten-second test: it plays your voice before and after kyrr.

Stays on your PC.

Your voice and your text never leave your computer. No cloud, no account, no tracking.

kyrr only goes online to fetch the speech model once and to check once a day for a new version (you can switch that off). Exactly what connects where

Download

Windows

Download for Windows

64-bit Windows 10 (22H2) or 11 on Intel or AMD. Not code-signed yet, so Windows may warn you: “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Why, and more

Start at sign-in

kyrr starts by itself when you sign in, once its first-start setup is done. To change that: Settings in kyrr, “Start when you sign in”.

Linux

Paste this into a terminal. It downloads kyrr and starts it:

mkdir -p ~/.local/bin
curl -L -o ~/.local/bin/kyrr https://github.com/heast13/kyrr/releases/latest/download/kyrr-x86_64.AppImage
chmod +x ~/.local/bin/kyrr
~/.local/bin/kyrr &

For current 64-bit distributions with PipeWire (Ubuntu 24.04+, Fedora, Arch and friends). Prefer clicking? Get the file, allow it to run as a program, double click. On first start your desktop may ask once for the dictation key and whether kyrr may type; kyrr walks you through it. More details: README

Uninstall

Quit kyrr (“Quit”, bottom left), then paste:

rm -f ~/.local/bin/kyrr \
  ~/.config/autostart/io.github.heast13.kyrr.desktop \
  ~/.local/share/applications/io.github.heast13.kyrr.desktop \
  ~/.local/share/icons/hicolor/256x256/apps/io.github.heast13.kyrr.png \
  ~/.local/share/icons/hicolor/scalable/apps/io.github.heast13.kyrr.svg
rm -rf ~/.config/kyrr ~/.local/share/kyrr

The last line also deletes your settings and the speech models. Leave it out to keep them.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

Only to download the speech model the first time you dictate, and for the daily check for a new version. Noise removal needs no download at all.

Do I need a fancy computer?

No. Any 64-bit PC with Intel or AMD will do, no graphics card needed. A small filter is there for older laptops. (Windows on ARM: dictation only.)

Which languages?

Dictation understands German and English. The noise filter works whatever language you speak.

Does it work on a Mac?

No, kyrr is for Windows and Linux.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It's free and open source, and there is no account to make.

How is it different from NVIDIA Broadcast or Krisp?

Broadcast needs an RTX graphics card, Krisp an account and, for full use, a subscription. kyrr needs neither, and it types what you say on top. As far as we know in October 2026; corrections welcome.

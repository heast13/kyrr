Noise suppression and dictation for Windows and Linux

kyrr is a free, open-source app. It removes background noise from your microphone and converts speech to text in German and English. All audio is processed on your computer.

Features

Noise suppression

Removes background noise such as typing, fans or traffic from the microphone signal in real time. kyrr uses the neural network DPDFNet, with a large model and a small one that needs less processing power. Apps such as Teams, Zoom, Discord, OBS or a browser receive the cleaned sound without any change to their settings.

Voice processing

Filter for low rumble, tone presets (Neutral, Warm, Clear, Bright or custom), compressor, de-esser, pop protection and automatic volume. Default: neutral tone, medium compression, light de-essing, rumble filter and automatic volume. Everything can be changed in Settings.

Dictation

Hold F9 while you speak. When you release it, kyrr types the text at the cursor, in the app you are typing in. German and English. “New line” inserts a line break. Instead of F9 you can use F8, Pause or right Ctrl, or tap once to start and once to stop.

Level check

Meters show what the microphone picks up and what apps receive. A ten-second test records your voice and plays it back before and after processing. You can also listen to yourself live on headphones.

How it works

  1. Installation. Windows: an installer, which needs administrator rights. Linux: a single AppImage file, no administrator rights. kyrr sets itself up on first start.
  2. Audio. On Windows, kyrr adds a filter to the Windows audio system that runs directly on the microphone. On Linux, kyrr creates a PipeWire microphone called “kyrr” and makes it the default. Your apps need no changes. If you switch to another microphone, kyrr switches with it.
  3. Dictation. The first time you dictate, kyrr downloads the speech model for your language once (German 900 MB, English 663 MB). After that, dictation works offline.
  4. Updates. kyrr checks once a day for a new version and tells you about it. On Windows, “Update” installs it. On Linux, quit kyrr and run the install commands again.

Privacy

Audio and text are processed only on your computer. kyrr needs no account and sends no usage data.

kyrr connects to the internet for three things: downloading the speech model once (each file checked against a SHA256 hash), a daily update check with GitHub (can be switched off in Settings), and downloading an update when you click “Update”. Details in the README

System requirements

Windows

  • Windows 10 (22H2) or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • Intel or AMD processor. On Windows on ARM, only dictation works.
  • Administrator rights for installing

Linux

  • 64-bit Intel or AMD, glibc 2.39 or newer: Ubuntu 24.04, Fedora 40, Debian 13, Linux Mint 22, Arch, or newer
  • PipeWire. If it is missing, kyrr offers to install it.
  • Noise suppression needs a processor with AVX2 (most from 2015 onwards).

Both: no graphics card needed. Disk space for the speech model: 663 MB (English) or 900 MB (German).

Windows

Download for Windows

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows a warning: click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Why it is not signed, and how to verify the file

Start at sign-in

kyrr starts automatically when you sign in, once its first-start setup is done. To change this: Settings in kyrr, “Start with Windows”.

Linux

Paste this into a terminal. It downloads kyrr to ~/.local/bin, makes it executable and starts it:

mkdir -p ~/.local/bin
curl -L -o ~/.local/bin/kyrr https://github.com/heast13/kyrr/releases/latest/download/kyrr-x86_64.AppImage
chmod +x ~/.local/bin/kyrr
~/.local/bin/kyrr &

Without a terminal: download the AppImage, allow it to run as a program in your file manager (right click, Properties) and double click it. On first start kyrr adds itself to your app menu. On GNOME 48 or newer and KDE the desktop asks once for the dictation key and once whether kyrr may type; kyrr explains both steps. Notes for other desktops

Start at sign-in

kyrr starts automatically when you sign in, once its first-start setup is done. To change this: Settings in kyrr, “Start when you sign in”.

Uninstall

Quit kyrr (“Quit”, bottom left), then paste:

rm -f ~/.local/bin/kyrr \
  ~/.config/autostart/io.github.heast13.kyrr.desktop \
  ~/.local/share/applications/io.github.heast13.kyrr.desktop \
  ~/.local/share/icons/hicolor/256x256/apps/io.github.heast13.kyrr.png \
  ~/.local/share/icons/hicolor/scalable/apps/io.github.heast13.kyrr.svg
rm -rf ~/.config/kyrr ~/.local/share/kyrr

The last line also deletes your settings and the speech models. Leave it out to keep them.

Questions

Does kyrr need an internet connection?

Only to download the speech model the first time you dictate, for the daily update check, which you can switch off, and to download an update when you click “Update”. Noise suppression works without internet.

Which apps get the cleaned sound?

On Windows, every app that uses the microphone. On Linux, every app that uses the default microphone, which kyrr sets to its own “kyrr” microphone.

Is there a Mac version?

No. kyrr is available for Windows and Linux.

How does kyrr compare with NVIDIA Broadcast and Krisp?

NVIDIA Broadcast needs an NVIDIA RTX graphics card. Krisp needs an account and is free for 60 minutes a day, with a subscription beyond that. Both work as a virtual microphone that you select in each app. kyrr needs no selection in the apps, because it filters the microphone itself on Windows and sets its own microphone as the default on Linux. It needs no account and includes dictation. As of October 2026; corrections welcome.

Is kyrr finished?

kyrr is in active development. Bug reports and suggestions are welcome on GitHub.
